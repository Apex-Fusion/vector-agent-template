# Gotchas

Specific traps this template's own build and mainnet proof run hit, kept here so you don't repeat them.

| Gotcha | What goes wrong | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Env contract** | `.env.example` lists four separate identity variables (`SUPPLIER_PRIV_KEY_HEX`, `SUPPLIER_PUB_KEY_HEX`, `SUPPLIER_PKH`, `SUPPLIER_ADDRESS`) from one `gen-keypair` run. Copy only the private key and leave the rest blank, and the agent boots fine but serves an empty public key on `/capability`: buyer-side receipt verification breaks silently, and claims get rejected `wrong_supplier` because the on-chain escrow's supplier hash never matches an empty `SUPPLIER_PKH`. | Map all four printed fields into `.env`, every time you regenerate a key. Don't hand-copy a subset. |
| **`docker compose restart` vs. recreate** | You edit `.env` (commonly to paste in `ADVERT_REF` after posting your advert), then run `docker compose restart`. The container comes back with the *old* environment: `restart` reuses the already-created container, it does not re-read `env_file`. | After any `.env` change, recreate instead: `docker compose -f deploy/docker-compose.yml up -d --force-recreate`, or `down` then `up`. |
| **Timeout nesting** | You set `SERVICE_TIMEOUT_MS` close to or above your advert's `ADVERT_MAX_PROCESSING_MS`. The agent refuses to boot for the `custom` capability rather than let you claim jobs you can't submit in time and forfeit your bond. | Keep `SERVICE_TIMEOUT_MS` at least 30 seconds under `ADVERT_MAX_PROCESSING_MS`. The boot error names both values; read it, it's telling you the exact headroom you're short. |
| **Neutral model strings** | The `--model` string you pass to `post-advert` is written on-chain and lands verbatim in every buyer's signed receipt. Naming the upstream vendor or model you're wrapping turns your listing into free advertising for your supply chain and undercuts the point of selling a product. | Name your product, not your supplier. `records.extract.myproduct.v1` / `my-product-v1`, not the underlying model's own name. |
| **The wire payload is an envelope, not your raw content** | The vendored escrow builders hash a canonical one-element message array, `canonicalize([{ content: <text>, role: "user" }])`, as the payload preimage: a buyer can't hand the chain a precomputed hash, only the bytes that produce one. Your `SERVICE_URL` receives that whole JSON envelope string as the POST body. Treat the body as bare user content and you'll parse `{"content":...,"role":"user"}` itself as if it were the content. | Parse the envelope: pull `content` (and `role`) out of the POST body before doing anything with it. See the README's "Plug in your software" section. |
| **Wallet coin selection can spend your own collateral** | Fund a wallet as one lump sum, or let its working balance run thin, and an ordinary transaction's coin selection can consume the UTxO you were counting on as collateral for a later Claim or Accept, with funds already locked in an escrow by the time you find out. | Fund every wallet as two UTxOs from the start and keep the working balance clear of the cushion. Full detail and the exact numbers: `docs/WALLET.md`. |

See `docs/LIFECYCLE.md` for timing (deliver-by, the accept window, the Release-path gap) and `docs/WALLET.md` for funding shape and fees.
